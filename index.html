About us

A small team obsessed with the details

BrightNest started in 2014 with two cleaners, one van and a simple promise: treat every home like it’s our own. Today our fully vetted, background-checked team serves hundreds of households across the Portland metro area.

We show up on time, bring everything we need, and follow a 50-point checklist on every visit. If something isn’t right, we come back and make it right — free.
